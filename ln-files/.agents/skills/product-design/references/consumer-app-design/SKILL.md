# Consumer App Design

This skill is intentionally lightweight for now. Use it to keep consumer-app design separate from marketing pages and enterprise product interfaces, then strengthen it as consumer-app-specific rules emerge.

## Current Guidance

- Do not apply marketing-page logic directly: the goal is not only first-visit persuasion.
- Do not apply enterprise dashboard logic directly: the experience may need emotion, habit, browsing, onboarding, or brand expression.
- Identify the user's recurring motivation: entertainment, learning, shopping, communication, progress, identity, convenience, or habit.
- Balance consistency with moments of brand expression. Repeated actions should be predictable, while onboarding, milestones, content discovery, and empty states may carry more personality.
- Design for mobile ergonomics, responsive states, accessibility, and clear navigation before adding visual flourish.

When the task is actually a public landing page for a consumer app, use `marketing-page-design`. When the task is an admin console or analytics surface for a consumer product, use `product-interface-design`.
